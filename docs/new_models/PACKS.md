# Packs split into individual models

Each multi-model pack from Downloads is split into its top-level objects (one cell per object, with name, triangles and raw size). Sizes are raw model units. Source list: [ALL_MODELS.md](ALL_MODELS.md); themed overview: [THEMES.md](THEMES.md). Per-pack `units.json` lists every object (node name, triangles, size).

Notes: `european_buildings_asset_pack_1` is a kit of loose facade/roof/wall pieces (196 parts), so its cells are parts, not whole buildings. Duplicate files (`buildings_pack (1)`, `low-poly_city_street_buildings_asset_pack (1)`) are not split again. Already split elsewhere: Strike Fighters packs (military catalog), city street pack (city_street_pack), vehicles and shops (themes 13-18).
